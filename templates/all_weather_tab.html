All-Weather Investing — one engine for every market

A regime-switching book, the way institutions run it: read the market state from the NIFTY trend, then switch engines — run offense in bull markets, rotate to Defensive Leaders when it turns choppy, and raise cash in a confirmed downtrend. Holdings are tilted toward QMJ-style fundamental quality (profitable, growing, well-managed) — the "flight-to-quality" factor that adds alpha and cushions drawdowns. The goal isn't to beat a raging index on raw return — it's to compound through every regime with a fraction of the drawdown. Mechanical & rule-based; not financial advice.
