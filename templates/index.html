⚡ Pocket Pivot (Gil Morales / Kacher)
Stock in uptrend (near 50-day MA) • Today closes UP • Today's volume exceeds every single down-day volume in prior 10 sessions • Not more than 15% above MA50 (fresh signal, not extended) • Indicates institutional buying absorbing supply.
