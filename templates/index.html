Vol SpikeUnusual volume against the 20-day median — often the first footprint of institutional interest.
Unusual Volume Scanner detects institutional accumulation by measuring how much today's volume deviates from the 20-day average (RVOL). Large volume with strong price action = smart money entering quietly.
🔴 Accumulation — big vol, closed near high, price held 🚀 Breakout — RVOL ≥ 2.5× + near 52W high 🟡 Stealth — moderate vol, tight candle, quietly absorbed 📈 Streak — 3+ consecutive above-avg vol days 🔵 VDU — Vol Dry-Up after spike, coiling for move

Unusual Volume Signals

Symbol ↕ RVOL ↕ Volume Day % ↕ Close Pos Streak Pattern From High 52W Pos Stage Sector Alert
Click Run Volume Scan to detect unusual volume signals.
How to read this scanner:
  • RVOL (Relative Volume) — today's volume ÷ 20-day average. RVOL 3× = 3× normal activity.
  • Close Pos — bar shows where price closed within the day's range. Green = near high (bullish), Red = near low (bearish).
  • 🔴 Accumulation — best pattern: high volume, closed near high, price held. Institutions loading quietly.
  • 🚀 Breakout — volume + price confirmation above resistance. Near 52W high adds conviction.
  • 🔵 VDU (Vol Dry-Up) — volume spiked recently then completely dried up. Stock is coiling. Breakout may be imminent.
  • Streak 3d+ — 3+ days in a row with above-avg volume = sustained institutional interest, not a one-day fluke.
  • ⚠️ Distribution — avoid. High volume + closed near low + price fell = institutions selling.

{{ DISCLAIMER }} Volume is end-of-day only; intraday spikes are not captured.

Score Validation — Does Setup Score Predict Forward Alpha?

Walk-forward Information Coefficient: at each historical snapshot we score every stock using ONLY data through that date, then measure forward 20-day excess return vs NIFTYBEES. Quintile bucketing across all observations + mean Spearman IC + t-stat. This answers the only question that matters: does a high score actually lead to high alpha, or is the score just dressing up noise?

Score Components — Which Raw Features Actually Predict Alpha?

Per-feature walk-forward IC: each candidate input (r3m, r6m, MA alignment, distance from 52W high, etc.) is measured INDIVIDUALLY against forward 20-day excess alpha. Identifies which inputs carry real signal and which are noise — the data-driven basis for choosing what to weight in the composite Setup Score.
